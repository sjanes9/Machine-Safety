Scope and administration

Identify the machine, the boundaries of the assessment and the people accountable for it. These fields print into the title block and the signature page of every output.

Photographs of the machine and cell

General views, the guarding, the layout and the access points. These print in the report and stay inside the saved file, so nothing is lost when it moves between people. Photos are shrunk on the way in.

Method

Task based risk assessment

One row per task and hazard pair. Score the initial risk as if no risk reduction measure existed, choose the measures in hierarchy order, then re-score the row. Acceptable risk is low or negligible.

Safety devices

The physical parts that carry out the safety functions: interlocks, light curtains, safety relays and controllers, contactors, valves and drives. Record each one once here with its safety values, then assign it to as many safety functions as it serves.

How to use this

Field verification

The final step, and a different question from the one before it. Step 4 asks whether the analysis is right. This asks whether the hardware bolted to the machine does what the analysis says it does. It is done device by device, on the installed equipment, with the machine in front of you.

An emergency stop is not proved by pressing it. It is proved by pressing it, then by removing channel A, then by removing channel B, because a device wired single channel passes the first step and fails the other two. Each step carries its own verdict for that reason.

Safeguarding checklist

Part of the same step: an observation of installed guarding, not an analysis. Four of these are access prevention questions, marked below, and they decide whether exposure can be scored as prevented anywhere on this file. A control system that reaches its required performance level does not prevent access on its own.

Guard opening measurements

Reach distances to ANSI B11.19. Given the width of an opening, the table gives how far the hazard has to sit behind it. Recording a measurement here is stronger than answering the reach question by eye, and a failed measurement blocks exposure prevented on its own.

Safety functions: requirements specification

Each safety function needs a written specification before it can be validated. State what triggers it, what it does, how fast, how it is reset, and which operating modes it applies in. Table 5 sets the minimum performance level and structure category from the initial risk level of every row that relies on it.

Verification and validation records

Clause 6.8 splits this in two. Verification of the risk assessment asks whether the measures chosen will achieve acceptable risk. Validation happens afterwards on the built cell and shall include both a physical test or inspection of the measures and a functional safety validation under ISO 13849-2. Each record names the method, the acceptance criterion, the measured result and the person who signed it.
